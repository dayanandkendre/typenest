Privacy Policy

Last Revised: September 2026 • Official Data Governance Document

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Welcome to TypeNest (https://typenest.in), an online touch-typing education portal operated and developed by Dayanand Dinkar Kendre, located in Moshi, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with applicable global privacy standards, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, processing, and storage of your information.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly limit data collection to what is necessary for delivering a functional, responsive typing platform:

  • Account & Profile Information: When signing in via Google Single Sign-On (SSO), we receive your verified email address, display name, and avatar picture provided by Google.
  • Typing Performance Analytics: We store aggregated speed metrics (Words Per Minute - WPM, Net WPM, accuracy percentages, lesson completion status, and optional public leaderboard ranks).
  • Client Keystroke Isolation: Real-time keystroke tracking during typing practice occurs purely on the client-side (inside your web browser's DOM memory). TypeNest does not record, log, or transmit raw keylogger inputs to any remote database.
  • Device & Log Telemetry: Standard non-identifying telemetry including browser engine type, screen resolution, operating system, and IP address for diagnostic and load-balancing purposes.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Authentication

Authentication and database hosting are provided through Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). User records and scores are stored within Google's enterprise-grade cloud centers adhering to strict ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 security compliances. TypeNest never accesses, stores, or processes plain-text account passwords.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising (Mandatory Policy)

TypeNest participates in the Google AdSense publisher network. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) enables Google and its authorized partners to serve tailored ads to our visitors.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by configuring their preferences in Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies across various ad networks by visiting www.aboutads.info or Your Online Choices (EU).

5. Cookies & Consent Management (CMP)

Cookies are compact text files stored on your local machine to enhance navigation. TypeNest utilizes cookies for:

  • Essential Functionality: Storing interface preferences (such as light/dark mode switch states and login sessions).
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) collects anonymized traffic metrics to evaluate page loading speeds and lesson usage trends.
  • Consent Mode v2: We respect Google Consent Mode v2 configurations, meaning analytical and advertising tags dynamically adjust based on your consent preferences.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR (For European Union Visitors)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you are entitled to statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: Request a complete export of your personal performance metrics.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate profile data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your Firebase user record and typing history.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke authorization for cookie tracking or profile indexing at any time.

7. Your Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (For California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents hold distinct privacy protections:

  • No Sale of Personal Information: TypeNest does not sell, rent, or trade personal data or practice records to data brokers or commercial marketers for monetary consideration.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never result in degraded test accuracy, locked courses, or denied platform access.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting the safety of children online is a paramount responsibility. TypeNest is an educational tool accessible to all ages; however, we do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without verified parental consent. If you believe your child has registered an account without consent, please contact us immediately to purge the record from our databases.

To request total account erasure, inspect your stored typing statistics, or ask questions regarding our privacy mechanisms, reach out directly to our Data Controller:
