Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Introduction

Welcome to TypeNest. We highly value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, manage, and safeguard your information when you practice and learn touch typing on our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to help you track your typing progress. This includes:

Account Data: When you log in via Google Sign-In, we securely receive your basic profile details, including your full name, email address, and profile picture.

Performance Data: We save your typing test scores, Words Per Minute (WPM), accuracy percentages, and course progress to display on your personal profile and leaderboard.

Authentication & Services

We use Firebase Authentication to handle user sign-ins safely. Your login credentials are managed entirely through Google's secure infrastructure, and TypeNest never sees or stores your personal passwords.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide a functional typing experience. This includes maintaining your account, updating the global leaderboard, saving your practice history, analyzing overall site performance, and ensuring platform security. We do not sell or lease your personal data to third parties.

Cookies and Analytics

TypeNest uses basic cookies to remember your display preferences (like dark mode) and keep you logged into your profile. We also utilize third-party services like Google Analytics to understand website traffic trends and optimize usability.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Ads

We partner with Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our site. Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve relevant ads based on your visits to TypeNest and other websites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Data Deletion & Your Rights

You retain full control over your information. You have the right to inspect your stored data, correct inaccuracies, or request the complete erasure of your account. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated typing history from our Firebase database, please contact us directly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data safety, please feel free to reach out to our team through the official Contact Us page on TypeNest.
