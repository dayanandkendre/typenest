Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026 • Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

TypeNest Academy ("TypeNest", "we", "our", or "us"), operating via https://typenest.in, respects user privacy. This policy details how we process user data, local storage preferences, third-party advertising cookies, and authentication records when you access our interactive typing arena, curriculum courses, and educational guides.

2. Information We Collect

TypeNest operates on a principle of minimal data collection. We only gather information strictly required to maintain performance statistics and authentication:

  • Authentication Information: When signing in through Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile parameters (display name, email address, and profile photo) managed by Google Firebase Authentication. We never access, handle, or store user account passwords.
  • Typing Performance Records: For authenticated typists, we log session metrics including Gross Words Per Minute (WPM), Net WPM, accuracy percentages, test durations, and timestamps to calculate personal profile progression and community leaderboard rankings.
  • Browser LocalStorage: Interface preferences such as visual themes (Dark/Light mode), keystroke audio feedback states, and offline drill progress are maintained locally within your browser.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and accredited third-party advertising partners across our editorial and guide pages:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its network partners to deliver relevant advertisements to our visitors based on their browsing behavior on TypeNest and other web properties.
  • Users can opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time by configuring their preferences in Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, visitors can opt out of third-party vendor tracking cookies for interest-based advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance at www.aboutads.info.

4. Cloud Storage & Infrastructure Security

User profile statistics and authenticated sessions are maintained using Google Cloud Firestore database infrastructure. Data is protected using strict server-side Firestore Security Rules to prevent unauthorized read/write access.

5. Children's Privacy & Advertising Restrictions

TypeNest Academy is an educational keyboarding platform designed for general audiences, students, and competitive test candidates. We observe strict advertising protections:

  • We do not knowingly direct commercial advertising personalization toward children under 13 years of age in accordance with Google Publisher policies and international standards.
  • Where Indian data protection requirements concerning minors apply under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, personal data of individuals recognized as children is handled strictly in adherence to statutory parental consent and data protection mandates.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that personal credentials have been submitted without appropriate authorization, they may reach out to our contact desk to initiate immediate deletion.

6. Data Subject Rights (DPDP Act 2023 Alignment)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and international privacy frameworks, users maintain full control over their personal data:

  • Right of Access & Summary: You can review your historical speed test sessions on your user profile dashboard at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to incorrect profile metadata.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account, leaderboard entries, and stored practice metrics by contacting us.
