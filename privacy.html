Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026 • Effective Date: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

TypeNest Academy ("TypeNest", "we", "our", or "us"), accessible via https://typenest.in, respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle personal data, cookies, authentication records, and user preferences when you interact with our typing practice arena and informational guides.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver typing performance tracking:

  • Account Credentials: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo via Google Firebase Authentication. We do not receive or store your Google passwords.
  • Typing Performance Metrics: We store test duration, Gross WPM, Net WPM, accuracy percentage, and test dates to render your profile statistics and public leaderboard rankings.
  • Local Browser Storage: Settings such as selected theme (Dark/Light mode) and sound preferences are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and affiliated third-party ad networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to TypeNest and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Data Storage & Security (Firebase)

All authenticated user data and leaderboard rankings are secured using Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure located in compliant enterprise data centers. We implement strict Firestore Security Rules preventing unauthorized modifications.

5. Children's Privacy

TypeNest does not knowingly collect identifiable personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal details through Google login without consent, please contact us immediately to have the record deleted from our database.

6. Compliance with Indian DPDP Act & Global Regulations

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and applicable privacy standards, users retain the right to access, rectify, or request complete erasure of their stored profile data and test history by contacting our support desk.
