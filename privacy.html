Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Effective: 2 October 2026.

1. Introduction

TypeNest Academy ("TypeNest", "we", "us") runs typenest.in. This policy explains what information we handle when you use our typing practice, speed tests, courses and guides, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

2. Information we collect

We try to collect only what is needed to run the site:

  • Sign-in information: If you sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication, we receive your display name, email address and profile photo. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Typing records: For signed-in users we store session results such as gross WPM, net WPM, accuracy, test duration and timestamps. They power your progress history and leaderboards, so your display name and scores may be visible to other users on leaderboards.
  • Browser storage: Preferences such as theme, keystroke sound settings and offline drill progress are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device.

3. Advertising and cookies

TypeNest may display ads from Google AdSense and other advertising partners. If ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to show ads to you based on your visits to TypeNest and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' interest-based advertising at aboutads.info.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as expected.

4. Third-party services

TypeNest uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign users in and store typing records. Pages also load resources from third-party hosts such as Google Fonts and content delivery networks (CDNs), which receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests them. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and security

Account and performance data is stored in Cloud Firestore, and we use Firestore Security Rules to restrict who can read and write it. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so we cannot promise that data will never be accessed without authorisation.

6. Retention

We keep account and typing records while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete them. Preferences stored in your browser stay on your device until you clear them.

7. Children

TypeNest is an educational site and is used by students. We do not knowingly want to collect personal data from children without the consent that applicable law requires. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, a child is a person under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data without your permission, email us and we will review and delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the DPDP Act, 2023. You can:

  • Access: View your past test results on your profile dashboard.
  • Correct: Ask us to correct inaccurate profile information.
  • Delete: Ask us to delete your account, leaderboard entries and stored practice records.

Send requests to the email address below. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version.
