How to Increase Typing Speed from 40 WPM to 80 WPM

8 min read • Typing Guide

Introduction

Many people start their typing journey at around 35 to 45 words per minute. This is considered an average typing speed for most computer users. While this speed is enough for basic browsing, emails, and occasional office work, it often becomes a limitation when dealing with long documents, coding projects, assignments, reports, or content writing.

The good news is that increasing your typing speed from 40 WPM to 80 WPM is completely achievable. In fact, thousands of students, professionals, programmers, and writers have doubled their typing speed by following proper techniques and maintaining consistent practice.

The biggest misconception about typing is that fast typists are naturally gifted. In reality, typing speed is a skill. Just like riding a bicycle or learning a musical instrument, it improves through practice, repetition, and correct habits.

This guide explains proven methods used by experienced typists to increase speed while maintaining excellent accuracy.

Understanding the Difference Between 40 WPM and 80 WPM

Before discussing improvement strategies, it helps to understand what the numbers actually mean.

A typist working at 40 WPM can type approximately 2,400 words per hour. At 80 WPM, that same person can type nearly 4,800 words per hour.

This means doubling your typing speed can save significant time every day. Over weeks and months, those saved minutes become hours of increased productivity.

Benefits of Reaching 80 WPM

  • Faster assignment completion.
  • Higher office productivity.
  • Better performance in typing tests.
  • Improved coding efficiency.
  • More natural writing flow.
  • Reduced frustration while working.

1. Master Proper Finger Placement

The foundation of fast typing begins with correct finger placement. Most slow typists develop habits that involve excessive finger movement and unnecessary hand repositioning.

Professional typists use the home-row method because it minimizes movement and maximizes efficiency.

Home Row Position

  • Left Hand: A, S, D, F
  • Right Hand: J, K, L, ;

The F and J keys contain small raised bumps that help you locate the correct position without looking at the keyboard.

When your fingers always return to the home row, muscle memory develops faster and typing becomes more automatic.

2. Focus on Accuracy Before Speed

One of the most common mistakes among beginners is trying to type faster before learning to type accurately.

Many learners constantly watch their WPM score and ignore mistakes. This approach usually creates bad habits that become difficult to correct later.

Typing speed grows naturally from accuracy. When your fingers consistently hit the correct keys, your brain develops stronger muscle memory patterns.

Why Accuracy Matters

  • Reduces corrections.
  • Builds confidence.
  • Improves rhythm.
  • Creates long-term speed gains.
  • Develops stronger muscle memory.

A typist with 98% accuracy at 60 WPM is often more productive than someone typing 80 WPM with frequent mistakes.

3. Stop Looking at the Keyboard

The habit of looking at the keyboard is one of the biggest barriers preventing people from becoming fast typists.

When you depend on visual guidance, your brain never fully develops muscle memory. Every glance at the keyboard slows your workflow and interrupts concentration.

Touch typing eliminates this dependency by teaching your fingers where each key is located.

Benefits of Touch Typing

  • Higher typing speed.
  • Improved concentration.
  • Better posture.
  • Reduced eye movement.
  • Greater confidence.

At first, your speed may temporarily decrease. This is normal and should not discourage you. The long-term benefits are worth the initial adjustment period.

4. Practice Every Day

Consistency is more important than intensity. Many learners practice for several hours on one day and then skip practice for an entire week.

Typing improvement happens through repeated exposure and reinforcement. Daily practice helps your brain strengthen typing patterns and improve finger coordination.

Recommended Daily Routine

  • 15–20 minutes per day.
  • 5–6 days each week.
  • Short focused sessions.
  • Regular progress tracking.

Even a small daily commitment can produce dramatic improvements over a period of several months.

5. Use All Ten Fingers

Many computer users rely on two-finger or three-finger typing. While this may seem comfortable, it significantly limits typing speed potential.

Professional typists use all ten fingers because it distributes work efficiently across both hands.

When multiple fingers share responsibility for different keys, movements become shorter and more efficient.

Advantages of Using All Ten Fingers

  • Higher maximum speed.
  • Less hand fatigue.
  • Improved accuracy.
  • Better typing rhythm.
  • Greater long-term efficiency.

Learning this technique may feel awkward initially, but it creates the foundation required for reaching 80 WPM and beyond.

6. Maintain Proper Typing Posture

Typing speed is not influenced only by finger movement. Your sitting posture also plays a significant role in comfort, endurance, and overall performance.

Many people develop poor typing habits because they sit too close to the screen, hunch their shoulders, or place their wrists in uncomfortable positions. Over time, these habits can lead to fatigue and reduced typing efficiency.

Ideal Typing Posture

  • Sit upright with your back supported.
  • Keep both feet flat on the floor.
  • Relax your shoulders.
  • Position your monitor at eye level.
  • Keep your wrists straight and relaxed.
  • Avoid excessive tension in your hands.

A comfortable posture allows your fingers to move naturally and efficiently, helping you maintain speed for longer periods.

7. Learn Common Word Patterns

Fast typists rarely think about individual letters. Instead, they recognize entire words and letter combinations automatically.

For example, words such as "the", "because", "typing", "practice", and "keyboard" become familiar movement patterns rather than separate keystrokes.

As your brain becomes familiar with these patterns, typing becomes faster and requires less conscious effort.

How to Practice Word Patterns

  • Type complete articles and paragraphs.
  • Practice commonly used English words.
  • Read regularly to improve word recognition.
  • Focus on frequently used phrases.

Over time, your fingers begin to move automatically when typing familiar words, significantly increasing your speed.

8. Take Regular Typing Tests

One of the best ways to improve typing speed is to measure your progress consistently.

Typing tests help identify weaknesses, track improvement, and provide motivation. Without testing, it becomes difficult to know whether your practice sessions are producing results.

Recommended Test Types

  • 1-Minute Test – Measures raw speed.
  • 3-Minute Test – Balances speed and accuracy.
  • 5-Minute Test – Measures endurance and consistency.

Try taking a typing test at least three times per week. Record your WPM and accuracy scores so you can monitor improvement over time.

Do not focus on a single score. Instead, observe long-term trends. Consistent progress is more important than occasional high scores.

9. Reduce Dependence on Backspace

Many typists lose significant time because they constantly use the Backspace key to correct mistakes.

While correcting errors is important, excessive Backspace usage usually indicates that accuracy needs improvement.

Fast typists maintain a steady rhythm and avoid unnecessary corrections whenever possible.

Ways to Reduce Mistakes

  • Slow down slightly during practice.
  • Focus on accuracy first.
  • Maintain a steady typing rhythm.
  • Think ahead while typing.
  • Avoid rushing through difficult words.

As accuracy improves, your need for corrections naturally decreases, resulting in smoother and faster typing.

10. Build Consistency and Patience

Perhaps the most important factor in reaching 80 WPM is consistency.

Many people start enthusiastically but quit after a few days because they expect immediate results. Typing improvement does not happen overnight.

Every practice session strengthens muscle memory and improves finger coordination. Small improvements accumulate over weeks and months.

Key Principles to Remember

  • Consistency beats intensity.
  • Accuracy creates speed.
  • Patience creates mastery.
  • Practice builds muscle memory.
  • Long-term habits produce lasting results.

30-Day Plan to Reach Higher Typing Speeds

Week 1

  • Learn proper finger placement.
  • Practice home-row positioning.
  • Focus entirely on accuracy.
  • Avoid looking at the keyboard.

Week 2

  • Take daily typing tests.
  • Track WPM and accuracy.
  • Improve weak keys and finger movements.
  • Use all ten fingers consistently.

Week 3

  • Practice complete paragraphs.
  • Type common words repeatedly.
  • Improve typing rhythm.
  • Reduce unnecessary corrections.

Week 4

  • Take longer typing tests.
  • Build endurance.
  • Focus on maintaining accuracy at higher speeds.
  • Continue daily practice.

By following this plan consistently, many learners see measurable improvements within the first month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to go from 40 WPM to 80 WPM?

For most learners, reaching 80 WPM takes between two and six months of consistent practice. Progress depends on current skill level, practice quality, and daily commitment.

Is 80 WPM considered fast?

Yes. An 80 WPM typing speed is considered above average and is sufficient for most professional tasks, academic work, and content creation.

Should I practice every day?

Yes. Daily practice produces better results than occasional long sessions because muscle memory develops through frequent repetition.

What is more important: speed or accuracy?

Accuracy should always come first. Speed naturally increases when accuracy improves.

Can adults improve typing speed?

Absolutely. Typing is a learnable skill, and adults can significantly improve their speed through proper practice and technique.

Conclusion

Increasing your typing speed from 40 WPM to 80 WPM is not about discovering shortcuts or secret techniques. It is about building efficient habits and practicing consistently.

By learning proper finger placement, focusing on accuracy, using all ten fingers, improving posture, practicing daily, and tracking progress, you can steadily increase your typing speed while maintaining excellent accuracy.

Remember that every fast typist started as a beginner. Stay patient, trust the process, and continue practicing regularly. With time and consistency, reaching 80 WPM can become an achievable and realistic goal.
