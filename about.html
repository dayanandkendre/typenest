About TypeNest Academy

Free, accessible tools for learning to type

TypeNest Academy is an independent platform for touch typing practice, typing ergonomics and speed evaluation, built for students, working professionals and anyone preparing for a typing test.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Our mission and background

TypeNest Academy was started in 2026 because many online typing tools work more like casual games than learning resources. We wanted a clean, distraction-free place to practise accuracy and steady rhythm, the habits that typing-based jobs and exams actually reward.

Our practice modules and timed tests are designed around the general format of common typing assessments, such as the Maharashtra GCC-TBC typing exams and Staff Selection Commission (SSC) typing tests, and they support bilingual layouts including Marathi Inscript. They are practice aids only. Always check the current rules, passage format and speed requirements published by the examining body before you sit an exam.

What you can do on TypeNest

  • Practise touch typing with structured exercises that build accuracy before speed.
  • Take timed speed tests and see your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy.
  • Train with arcade drills that make repetition less tedious.
  • Read guides on posture, hand position and speed-building techniques.

Results are practice indicators. Your speed depends on your keyboard, your practice habits and the text you type, and TypeNest cannot guarantee any particular WPM, exam result or job outcome.

Our standards

Editorial and technical care

Guides and tutorials are written with reference to publicly available exam information and general typing and ergonomics guidance. They are educational and are not medical, legal or official examination advice. If you find an error, tell us and we will review it.

Free to use

We treat touch typing as a basic modern skill, so the exercises, arcade drills and speed tests are free to use with no subscription or fee. The site may show advertising in future to help cover running costs; it will not replace or hide the learning content.

Independence

TypeNest Academy is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any government department, examination board or testing authority, and it does not issue official certification.
