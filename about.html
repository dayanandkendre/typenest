🚀 Our Mission

At TypeNest, our mission is to eliminate typing friction for students, competitive exam aspirants (such as GCC-TBC candidates), programmers, and working professionals. We believe that typing is a baseline cognitive multiplier: the faster and more accurately you translate thought into keystrokes, the more productive you become. TypeNest delivers high-precision, distraction-free touch typing tools completely free of charge.

👤 Founder & Technical Lead

TypeNest was founded and architected by Dayanand Dinkar Kendre, a web application developer and quality engineering professional based in Moshi (Khed, Pune), Maharashtra. Having worked extensively with software interfaces, technical workflows, and data accuracy, Dayanand identified the need for a modern, responsive typing platform tailored to students and professionals striving to cross the 60+ WPM benchmark without ergonomic fatigue.

🧠 Pedagogical Approach & Muscle Memory

Unlike basic typing tests that generate arbitrary random words, TypeNest organizes practice routines around neuromuscular motor control. Lessons isolate the Home Row, Top Row, Bottom Row, and Number rows before introducing compound sentences. We apply international stenography calculation standards: 5 keystrokes count as one standard word, distinguishing clearly between Gross WPM, Net WPM, and accuracy penalties.

📚 Editorial Standards & Original Content

Every tutorial, layout guide (QWERTY, Dvorak, Colemak), and ergonomic post published on TypeNest is independently authored or vetted by our team. We do not host auto-generated thin summaries. Our resources cover real-world biomechanics—such as carpal tunnel prevention, correct wrist alignment, and tactile keyboard actuation—to promote safe, long-term typing habits.

📍 Base of Operations & Transparency

TypeNest operates independently out of Pune, Maharashtra, India. We are committed to complete user transparency: we do not sell keystroke data, practice logs are stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure, and all platform features remain open and accessible without paywalls.
