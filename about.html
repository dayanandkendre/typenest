About TypeNest Academy

Empowering Typists with Free, Accessible Tools

TypeNest is an independent touch typing practice and speed-building platform built for students, professionals, and government typing exam candidates across India and worldwide.

Our Mission & Background

Founded in 2026, TypeNest Academy was established to solve a common barrier faced by competitive exam aspirants: the lack of clean, non-distracting, and syllabus-aligned typing drill environments. Traditional practice sites often focus purely on casual English speed races, ignoring specialized test formats required by competitive commissions.

Our curriculum and evaluation drills cater specifically to regional and national typing standards, including Maharashtra GCC-TBC examinations, Staff Selection Commission (SSC) skill tests, High Court clerical exams, and bilingual keyboard layouts like Marathi Inscript.

Editorial & Curriculum Standards

Every guide and technical tutorial on TypeNest is authored and cross-verified against official exam notifications (such as MSCE Pune guidelines) and verified ergonomic research to ensure practical, reliable instruction.

100% Free & Open Access

We believe foundational digital literacy and touch typing muscle memory should be freely accessible to every student without paywalls, subscriptions, or restrictive sign-up demands.
