1. Inscript (Mangal) vs. Remington (Kruti Dev / ISM)

Aspiring government typists in Maharashtra frequently encounter confusion between legacy typewriter keyboards and modern Unicode layouts. Choosing the right layout from day one saves hundreds of practice hours:

Feature Inscript Layout (Mangal Font) Remington Layout (Kruti Dev / ISM)
Standardization Government of India Standard (BIS) Based on manual mechanical typewriters
Unicode Compliance 100% Native Unicode (Web, Mobile & Docs) Legacy ASCII (Requires font conversion)
GCC-TBC Acceptance Mandatory & Preferred across all centers Available in select commercial institutes
Learning Logic Vowels on Left Hand, Consonants on Right Arbitrary character placements

2. Maharashtra GCC-TBC Speed Criteria

The Maharashtra State Council of Examination (MSCE) conducts the Government Commercial Certificate in Typing Basic Course (GCC-TBC). To clear the Marathi examination, candidates must satisfy the following thresholds:

  • Marathi 30 WPM: 300 words typed within 10 minutes (Total 1,500 keystrokes). Minimum qualifying score is 50%.
  • Marathi 40 WPM: 400 words typed within 10 minutes (Total 2,000 keystrokes) with rigorous accuracy penalties.
  • Negative Marking: Each spelling error, skipped word, or wrong matra deducts marks directly from the final aggregate.

3. Inscript Scientific Key Mapping: The Left-Right Division

Unlike QWERTY English layouts, Inscript was linguistically engineered by CDAC for Indian phonetic scripts:

Left Hand: Vowels & Matras (स्वर आणि मात्रा)

Your left-hand fingers control all independent vowels (अ, आ, इ, ई, उ, ए) and dependent signs (ा, ि, ी, ु, ू, े, ै, ो, ौ). Pressing Shift unlocks independent vowel forms.

Right Hand: Consonants (व्यंजने)

Your right-hand fingers manage the primary consonant groups (क, ख, ग, घ, च, छ, ज, ट, ठ, त, थ, प, फ). Unshifted keys type unaspirated stops, while Shift reveals aspirated counterparts.

4. Typing Complex Conjuncts (जोडाक्षरे) Using the Halant (्)

5. Matras, Anuswar & Virama Key Shortcuts

  • Kana (ा): Key e (e.g., क + e = का)
  • First Velanti (ि): Key f (e.g., क + f = कि)
  • Second Velanti (ी): Key r (e.g., क + r = की)
  • Ukar (ु / ू): First ukar is key g; second ukar is key t
  • Anuswar (ं): Key x (e.g., क + x = कं)
  • Nukta (़): Key ] (used for Marathi ळ and Persian loan sounds)

6. Bombay High Court & District Court Typing Preparation

Court clerk and stenographer examinations in Maharashtra enforce stringent qualifying norms. You will be provided printed legal Marathi passages containing formal legal terminology (उदा. अर्जदार, प्रतिवादी, न्यायदंडाधिकारी).

Court Typist Practice Checklist:

  • Practice copying from printed physical paper sheets placed beside the keyboard (copy typing).
  • Eliminate frequent backspace usage to maintain continuous rhythmic cadence.
  • Train at least 5 WPM above the qualifying benchmark (target 35 WPM for 30 WPM exams).

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I switch from Remington to Inscript easily?

Yes. Because Inscript follows a structured vowel/consonant linguistic logic, most typists adapt fully within 10 to 14 days of dedicated practice.

Which font is officially used in GCC-TBC?

MSCE GCC-TBC computer-based examinations officially employ standard Unicode fonts such as Mangal, Nirmala UI, or Arial Unicode MS.
