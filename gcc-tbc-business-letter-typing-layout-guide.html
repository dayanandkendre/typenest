In the Maharashtra State GCC-TBC (Government Commercial Certificate in Basic Course) practical typing exam, candidates must complete three tasks within the allocated practical session: the speed passage (20 marks), the spreadsheet statement (15 marks), and the Business Letter (15 marks). While speed passage testing gauges pure velocity, the business letter evaluates clerical competence—specifically page composition, indentation rules, and conventional workplace correspondence layouts.

As discussed in our broader GCC-TBC Typing Exam Comprehensive Guide, candidates frequently drop marks on the business letter not due to slow typing, but because of incorrect spacing, misaligned complimentary closes, or improper line feeds. 01. 15-Mark Business Letter Blueprint

The business letter assessment evaluates typographical and layout proficiency across specific marking criteria:

01 / Text Accuracy (7 Marks)

Zero Spelling & Substitution Errors

Typing the company heading, recipient addresses, subject lines, body paragraphs, and signatory blocks without omitting words or mistyping names.

02 / Alignment & Margins (4 Marks)

Structural Indentation Rules

Maintaining exact 1.0 or 1.25-inch left/right margins, center alignment of the letterhead, and correct positioning of the date line and sign-off.

03 / Line Spacing & Paragraphs (2 Marks)

Vertical Spacing Compliance

Using single line spacing (1.0) within address blocks and double spacing (one blank line) between distinct sections and paragraphs.

04 / Punctuation & Salutation (2 Marks)

Conventional Case Discipline

Following capitalization conventions for reference numbers, salutations (Dear Sir/Madam,), subject headings, and enclosure notices.

02. Blocked vs. Semi-Blocked Layouts

Depending on the examination paper instructions, candidates are asked to type either a Fully Blocked Style or an Indented / Semi-Blocked Style letter. Understanding the distinction between the two is vital:

Feature Fully Blocked Style Semi-Blocked / Indented Style
Letterhead / Header Centered at the top of the page Centered at the top of the page
Date & Reference No. Flush Left Margin Date at Right Margin, Ref at Left
Inside Address Flush Left Margin Flush Left Margin
Paragraph Starts Flush Left (0 indent, blank line between) Indented by 0.5 inch (Tab key)
Complimentary Close & Sign Flush Left Margin Aligned to Center / Right (Tab to 3.5")

03. Page Setup, Margins & Spacing Rules

Before typing the first word of the letter, you must configure the document canvas properly in your word processor. Examiners deduct layout marks if the document overflows to a second page or has asymmetric margins:

  • Paper Size: Standard A4 (210 x 297 mm).
  • Margins: Top: 1.0 inch; Bottom: 1.0 inch; Left: 1.25 inches (standard filing margin); Right: 1.0 inch.
  • Standard Font: Times New Roman (12pt) or Arial (11pt) with 1.0 line spacing. Avoid mixing multiple typefaces.
  • Paragraph Spacing: Set Before and After spacing to 0 pt in paragraph settings to prevent unintentional extra gaps when pressing Enter.

05. Common Deductions & How to Prevent Them

Reviewing mistake patterns identified in our Typing Mistakes & Fixes Guide reveals the most frequent errors that cost candidates marks in letter exams:

  1. Missing Commas in Addresses: Omitting the trailing comma at the end of address lines (e.g., Apex Engineering Pvt. Ltd. without the required comma). In formal exam marking, missing punctuation qualifies as a half mistake.
  2. Inconsistent Date Formatting: Writing 15/10/26 when the question paper specifies 15th October, 2026. Always reproduce the date format exactly as given on the examination question sheet.
  3. Spacing with the Spacebar Instead of Tabs: Pressing the spacebar 20 to 30 times to push the date or complimentary close across the page. This causes jagged alignments. Always use proper tab stops (Tab key) for right-side elements.
  4. Overflowing onto Page Two: Having two lines of the signatory block spill onto a second page because paragraph spacing wasn't set to 0pt. All GCC-TBC business letters must fit cleanly on a single A4 page.

Conclusion

Securing a perfect 15 out of 15 on the GCC-TBC business letter section requires combining mechanical typing accuracy with clean structural formatting. By configuring margins first, relying on tab stops instead of manual spacebar taps, and proofreading punctuation blocks carefully, you can complete the letter within 8 to 10 minutes with zero deductions.
